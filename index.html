Hunter Road Force® Balancing

Vibration-free driving, finally

Conventional balancers only spin the wheel. Road Force simulates the weight of the road pressing on your tire to find what others miss — the perfect match for low-profile tires on European cars.

  • Detects tire and rim uniformity problems
  • Eliminates steering-wheel shimmy at highway speeds
  • Extends tire life and protects suspension parts
